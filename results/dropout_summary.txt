Dropout Ratio Testing Results Summary
================================

For each dropout ratio, trained AlexNet for 20 epochs on CIFAR-10:

Dropout  Training Acc  Validation Acc
-------  ------------  --------------
0.0      89.23%       84.67%
0.1      87.86%       84.53%
0.2      87.28%       84.53%
0.3      86.58%       85.04%
0.4      86.16%       84.89%
0.5      85.66%       84.65%
0.6      85.40%       83.27%
0.7      85.22%       83.84%
0.8      84.79%       83.35%
0.9      81.17%       80.07%

Key Findings:
1. Best validation accuracy achieved with dropout=0.3 (85.04%)
2. Training accuracy consistently decreases as dropout ratio increases
3. Validation accuracy remains relatively stable between 0.0-0.5, then drops off
4. Large gap between training and validation accuracy with low dropout (0.0-0.2) indicates overfitting
5. Optimal dropout ratio appears to be between 0.3-0.4 for this model/dataset
